Your customers live in the text thread. They check their phones 10+ times a day and send 32 texts for every 6 calls. When they need a quote, a repair, or a showing, they text first.

| Metric | SMS / Text | Email | Phone |

Every speed-to-lead rule your business already knows applies to text — amplified, because texts get *seen* in minutes, not hours.

Each minute of delay in that first window cuts qualification odds by 10%. InsideSales.com adds the killer stat: **78% of customers buy from the first company that responds.**

Now apply that to text:

| Response Window | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Under 5 minutes | You're first in the thread — 21x qualification advantage |
| 5–30 minutes | Odds drop ~50% from peak |
| 1 hour | Qualification odds down 10x |
| 47 hours (the national average) | The job is gone |

That 47-hour figure is real: GreetNow and HubSpot both clock the average business response time at **47 hours**. Your competitor isn't beating you on price or quality — they're beating you because they text back while you're still looking at a voicemail icon.

## FAQ

**What is an AI text agent?**
An AI text agent is an autonomous conversational system that receives, understands, and replies to customer text messages on your business's behalf. Unlike an autoresponder, it holds multi-turn conversations, remembers context, qualifies leads, and books appointments — in seconds, around the clock.

**How fast should a business respond to a text message?**
Within minutes — ideally under five. MIT/Harvard research shows leads contacted within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to be qualified than those contacted after 30 minutes. The average business takes 47 hours, which is effectively a forfeit.

**Do customers really prefer texting over calling?**
Yes. Roughly 90% of consumers say they'd rather text a business than call, and 53% call SMS their preferred communication channel. Meanwhile, about 80% of people don't answer calls from unknown numbers — the channels most businesses rely on are the ones customers avoid.

**Can an AI text agent actually book appointments?**
Yes. It checks your live calendar, offers available slots, books the appointment, and sends confirmations and reminders. SMS reminders alone reduce missed appointments by roughly 80%.

**Do AI text agents work after hours?**
That's their whole point. AI text agents run 24/7, and 52% of all sales leads arrive outside business hours. Businesses using AI capture dramatically more of those after-hours conversations than voicemail-reliant competitors.

**What does a missed text message cost a small business?**
At a $300–$900 average ticket, every unanswered textable inquiry is a conversation handed to a competitor. With 1,000+ textable inquiries a year at typical volumes, slow or missing text response quietly costs Metro Detroit businesses tens of thousands of dollars annually.

**Will an AI text agent replace my team?**
No — it handles the routine 80%: first response, qualification, FAQs, booking. Complex or sensitive conversations escalate to your team mid-thread with full context. Your people do the high-value work; the agent does the repetition.

**How do AI text agents integrate with existing systems?**
They plug into your CRM, calendar, and dispatch workflows. Every conversation is logged to the contact record, triggers automations, and updates deal stages — no fragmented threads, no lost details.
