## Why Commercial Real Estate Visuals Matter Differently

Residential drone photography is about making a home look inviting. Commercial drone photography serves a different purpose entirely — it's about proving a property's **functional and financial potential.**

A commercial buyer or tenant evaluates a property on fundamentally different criteria than a homebuyer:

**Location context matters more.** A retail tenant needs to see adjacent traffic patterns, anchor tenants, and access points from major roads. Ground photos can't convey that — only an aerial perspective can.

**Scale is the story.** A 50,000-square-foot industrial warehouse or a 12-acre development parcel looks incomplete in ground photos. From the air, the full footprint, setbacks, and expansion potential become immediately obvious.

**Parking is a deal-maker.** Commercial tenants — medical offices, retail, service businesses — evaluate parking ratios before square footage. Aerial photography shows the full parking field, ingress/egress points, and overflow capacity in a single frame.

**Roof condition is latent liability.** For industrial and office properties, the roof is a major capital expenditure item. Aerial photography documents roof condition without a ladder — and creates a timestamped visual record for due diligence.

**Signage visibility is revenue.** For retail and medical properties, street visibility and monument signage are directly tied to tenant revenue. A drone shot at street elevation + aerial reveals exactly how visible a property is from the roadway.
