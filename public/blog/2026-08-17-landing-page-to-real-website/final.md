## What a Landing Page Can and Can't Do

Landing pages aren't bad. They're just not a website.

| | Landing page | Real website |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Convert a single ad or campaign | Prove who you are and what you do |
| **Pages** | One | Multiple: services, about, proof, contact |
| **Proof** | A form | Work, results, testimonials, licensing |
| **Search** | Little to none | Local SEO, service pages, content |
| **Trust** | Built by the ad, not the page | Built by the site itself |
| **Life span** | A campaign | The life of your business |

The moment a prospect lands on your page, they ask a question you never get to answer in person: *is this business real?* A landing page answers with a headline and a form. A professional website answers with evidence.

## What Prospects Decide in Milliseconds

The research here is brutal and it's all on your side if you act on it:

- **75% of consumers judge a company's credibility based on website design alone** — Stanford Web Credibility research
- **94% of first impressions are design-related, not content-related** — visitors decide whether they trust you *before* reading a single word
- **Visitors form that first impression in about 50 milliseconds**
- **Over 60% of web traffic is mobile** — your site is now judged on a phone screen first
- **28% of small businesses still have no website at all — and businesses with one earn roughly 39% more revenue**

Read those numbers the way a decision-maker should: prospects aren't evaluating your services when they land on a thin page. They're evaluating whether you're worth calling. A dated, single-page presence tells a 20-year business with a 5-star reputation the same story it tells a weekend side project — *"we haven't invested in this."*

Here's what that mismatch costs:

Your reputation earned the opportunity. Your web presence is what converts it.

## What a Real Website Actually Includes

A professional web presence isn't a prettier page. It's a proof system:

1. **Services that are specific** — what you do, for whom, and why it matters (not a tagline)
2. **Proof you can show** — completed work, results, testimonials, licensing, credentials
3. **A real contact path** — phone, email, location, and a form that goes somewhere
4. **Trust signals** — reviews, guarantees, insurance, professional memberships
5. **Content that answers questions** — the things prospects search before they call
6. **Local SEO done properly** — the "near me" and service searches that feed your pipeline
7. **Fast, mobile-first performance** — because 60%+ of your visitors are on a phone

Notice what's missing: no fancy animations, no buzzwords, no "we're not your average agency" copy. Just evidence. That's the whole job — and it's the job most landing pages never do.
